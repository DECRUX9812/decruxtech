IT support in Regina, and around it.

Regina is small enough that reputation travels and large enough that nobody has spare time for a technician who returns calls when they feel like it. Here is what local support actually gets you, and what a visit looks like.

01

What "local" actually buys you.

Not a flag on a map. Three practical things that a national helpdesk cannot give a fifteen-person firm on Albert Street.

03

Who we sit down with in this city.

Regina's small-business mix is particular: clinics and dental practices, trades running out of trucks, professional firms with client files, and nonprofits stretched thin.

1 to 50 people
  • Clinics and dental practices needing HIPA-aware access control and a backup they have actually restored from.
  • Trades losing quotes and photos across three phones, needing one job record from first call to paid invoice.
  • Professional firms with former staff still in the account list and client documents outside the approved system.
  • Nonprofits rebuilding after the volunteer who set everything up moved away.

If you are none of those, the work is usually still the same shape: three disciplines, one written record, no lock-in.

04

Local questions.

Straight answers
Do you come on site in Regina? +
Yes. Anything physical (hardware, network, printers, new desks) is done in person in Regina, usually within one business day. Everything else is handled remotely and immediately.
Do you cover towns outside Regina? +
Yes, by arrangement: White City, Emerald Park, Pilot Butte, Lumsden and Moose Jaw are all practical. Remote support has no distance limit inside Saskatchewan.
Do you work with SaskTel or Rogers internet? +
Both, and with whatever circuit you already have. We deal with the ISP on your behalf when a line, modem or static IP is the problem.
Can you support a clinic with patient records? +
Yes. Saskatchewan clinics fall under HIPA rather than PIPEDA, so access logging, named accounts and tested backups are part of the setup rather than an optional extra.
What is the response time? +
A human reply within one business day for inquiries, and same-business-day triage for clients on a monthly plan. Urgent outages are handled by phone first.
